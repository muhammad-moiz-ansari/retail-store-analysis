\section{Floor Calibration Schema and Failure States}
The floor process is shown in Figure~\ref{fig:floor-pipeline}; actual preview, calibration and map examples are in Figures~\ref{fig:floor-preview}--\ref{fig:floor-map}. The authoritative correspondence order for the provisional rectangle is A--B--D--C. Floor coordinates are $(0,0)$, $(w,0)$, $(w,d)$ and $(0,d)$ in metres for that order.

\begin{tabular}{P{0.28\textwidth}P{0.59\textwidth}}
\toprule
Record group & Key information\\
\midrule
Identity / geometry & Schema version, camera/calibration ID, source reference, frame index/timestamp, frame size, coordinate space.\\
Landmarks / mapping & Original-pixel and metre correspondences, authoritative dimensions, image-to-floor H, inliers/residuals.\\
Operational quality & Supported polygon, exclusions, distortion handling, motion thresholds, smoothing/prediction limits.\\
Measurement status & Estimated calibration inputs and approximate-output metadata.\\
Runtime observation & Track ID, role/score, pixel foot proxy, nullable floor coordinates, motion/position state and reasons.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

States include \texttt{uncalibrated}, \texttt{observed\_proxy}, \texttt{predicted}, \texttt{unreliable}, \texttt{out\_of\_region}, \texttt{motion\_unverified} and \texttt{motion\_invalid}. Only supported observed proxies contribute to approximate pair distances. The localization equations are given in Equations~\ref{eq:floor-foot}--\ref{eq:floor-distance}; no 3D reconstruction is implied. These records belong to the descoped floor prototype (Section~\ref{sec:floor-descoped}).

\section{Interaction Verdict States}
Each interaction candidate in the current pipeline carries one verdict; annotated output is in Figure~\ref{fig:judge-examples}.

\begin{tabular}{P{0.22\textwidth}P{0.65\textwidth}}
\toprule
Verdict & Meaning\\
\midrule
\texttt{interaction} & The pose judge found a head turned towards the other person in at least half of the judged frames.\\
\texttt{no\_interaction} & Poses were found but nobody faced the other person; written to \texttt{.rejected\_interactions.json} and re-judged every four seconds while the pair stays close.\\
\texttt{unverified} & Judge switched off, no pose found, or the judge failed; the proximity result stands.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

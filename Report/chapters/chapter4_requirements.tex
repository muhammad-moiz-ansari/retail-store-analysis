\section{Scope and Identifier Policy}
The active baseline contains exactly 17 functional capabilities, each with one primary use case using the same numerical suffix. Gaps preserve identifier continuity: retired identifiers are not reused for unrelated capabilities. Requirements specify intended behaviour, while the implementation/evidence column distinguishes source inspection, dated execution and proposed work. The consolidation and retired prototype are recorded in Appendix~\ref{app:req-history}.

The target architecture uses Customer Behavior Agent, Employee Activity Agent, Interaction Agent, Employee Task Assignment Agent, Insight Narration Agent, Query Agent and Orchestrator. Detection, role classification, tracking, identity association, geometry and pose/proximity are shared tools. Employee Activity consumes interaction context; Interaction does not depend on Employee Activity's final output. Direct assignment goes to identified employee accounts without a manager approval gate. An observed employee track is not automatically a roster/account identity.

NFR-01 & Repeated numerical calculations with the same inputs/configuration shall agree within a documented tolerance. & Reuse input, model/version and configuration metadata and compare repeated numerical outputs against a stated tolerance; broader pipeline repeatability remains to be profiled. Verify corrupt inputs, model failures and interrupted exports under UC-01 rather than interpreting an error as an empty successful detection.\\
NFR-03 & Main analytics pages shall be reachable within two navigation actions from the defined role landing page. & Use direct role-appropriate navigation links already present, and define the starting workspace before counting actions. NFR-04 & Charts shall show textual values and readable consistent fonts. & Use the existing Space Grotesk, Inter and JetBrains Mono theme with chart labels and textual metric values; extend incomplete views consistently. TC-45 will check clipping/readability at documented viewport sizes.\\
NFR-05 & Vision processing shall support CPU-only hardware with configurable sampling. & Use the existing lightweight detector/OpenVINO path and configurable scoring/tracking intervals, treating processing as sampled/offline where necessary. Recorded benchmark throughput is configuration-specific; TC-46 will verify speed and environment without promising an unmeasured real-time rate.\\
NFR-06 & Dashboard pages shall provide acceptable responsiveness on a stated laptop/environment; no supervisor-approved numeric target exists. & Existing pages load data asynchronously; bounded interval queries and aggregate retrieval are proposed for heavier analytics. TC-46 will measure page latency separately from compilation, inference and the recorded warm-login HTTP timings; a numeric acceptance threshold remains unspecified.\\
NFR-07 & Protected manager functions shall require an authenticated Manager and prevent role escalation. & Use signed HttpOnly sessions and server-side role checks on protected endpoints; the client route guard supplies navigation behaviour rather than sole data protection. TC-01/02 record subsets, while additional invalid/expired-session and missing-route checks remain required.\\
NFR-08 & Protected backend access shall be restricted to authorized application calls. & Existing protected routes validate an internal secret and the Next.js server proxy adds the secret and actor context without exposing it to browser code. TC-02 records missing/wrong-secret rejection; verify remaining protected routes and alternate camera-secret paths separately.\\
NFR-09 & Secrets shall be configured securely and excluded from published source/logs. & Use environment lookups already supported and maintain placeholder-only examples and excluded real secret files; complete configuration/history review remains required. TC-47 is a planned redacted audit of examples, logs and browser-delivered configuration, not a claim that every current secret is compliant.\\
NFR-10 & No biometric identifiers shall be stored. & Limit retained operational data and avoid facial-identity templates; existing appearance descriptors/embeddings, identity associations and retention behaviour require inspection and minimisation before compliance is asserted. TC-48 will audit those paths and access/retention controls; temporary track IDs are not assumed automatically anonymous.\\
